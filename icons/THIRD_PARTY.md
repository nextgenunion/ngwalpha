General interface symbols are sourced from Google Material Icons (Apache 2.0). Existing attributed Material Symbols and Simple Icons assets are retained. The brand note and Sabbath mascot remain artwork, not generic interface symbols. AI authorship cannot be inferred from SVG path syntax.

Google: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
Simple Icons: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
